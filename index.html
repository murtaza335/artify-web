ARTIFY

Endless shard art. Every tap breaks one picture apart and builds something new from the pieces. Solve it as a puzzle, throw paint, or build your own design.

Get Artify for your editor

The same art lives in the Artify extension for VSCodium, Cursor, Windsurf and other editors that use Open VSX, along with two colour themes.
